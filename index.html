Buyer's Guide: Selecting the Right Poultry Box

Whole Bird vs Portion Cut Boxes

Whole bird processing boxes are typically deeper (300–400 mm internal depth) with a wider footprint to accommodate extended carcasses. Portion cut boxes tend to be shallower (150–250 mm) with closer-tolerance stacking features so they can stack 8–10 high on a processing table without lateral shift.

If your facility handles both whole birds and portioned product, consider specifying boxes that can be used at both stages — a medium-depth box that accommodates whole small birds and stacks efficiently when holding portioned cuts reduces the number of container SKUs you need to manage.

Live vs Processed Product

Ventilated poultry boxes (with perforated or slatted walls) are required for live bird transport to maintain airflow and meet animal welfare regulations. Processed product boxes use solid or minimal-perforation walls for hygiene and temperature retention. Do not use live bird transport crates for processed product — the perforation pattern creates cleaning difficulty and the surface finish is not optimised for food contact hygiene.

Palletising and Cold Store Racking

For cold store rack systems, confirm the beam span and load-per-beam with Elipacko before ordering. Poultry box designs intended for floor-stacking may not have the base runner structure needed to span racking beams under load. The right base specification will be confirmed at the quoting stage.
